# Design: The Front Door

**Command:** `/dumdum-designer:design`

## What It Does and Why

You shouldn't have to learn five commands to design one page. Type `/dumdum-designer:design` followed by what you want, like `/dumdum-designer:design the member dashboard`, and Dumdum works out the rest.

It quietly looks at what you already have: a brand, a plan for this page, an earlier prototype, or a real app with similar pages. Then it tells you, in two or three lines, the route it will take. For example: first your look, then a few questions about the page, then clickable versions to compare. Once you say OK, it takes you through each step in order, without you typing another command.

If you're not sure what you want, it explains the options in plain words and lets you pick.

## What You Get

Whatever the route produces: a brandbook, a page plan, clickable prototypes, a new version of an existing page, or a design check. Every step follows the same rules as running it directly. Your brand comes first, existing pieces are reused, every page is checked in a real browser, and your real project is never touched.

## Good to Know

- You can also just describe what you want without a slash command, like "design the member dashboard". Claude should pick this skill up.
- The other commands still work directly if you want to jump to one step.
