# Change It: Improve Your Prototype in Plain Words

**Command:** `/dumdum-designer:change-it`

## What It Does and Why

The first version of a page is never the last. This skill lets you change your prototype by just saying what you want, like "bigger headline", "move the prices higher" or "it feels too busy".

Vague feedback is fine. If you say "make it pop", Dumdum offers two or three concrete ideas of what that could mean, and you pick one. Before changing anything, it lists exactly what it will change, and it only changes what you asked for.

If you ask for something outside your brand, like a color your brand doesn't use, it explains your options: use it on just this page, add it to your brand everywhere, or use the closest brand color.

## What You Get

- A new version of your page, like `v4.html`, opened in your browser, with a short list of what changed.
- Every older version is kept. Say "go back to the last one" and it brings that version back as a new version, so your history stays complete.

## Good to Know

- It never edits an older version, and never edits your real app.
- If your project uses git, each version is saved on a separate throwaway branch, never on the branch you're working on.
